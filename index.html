Turn on mic makes the drawing sound-reactive: whatever is playing in the room pushes it around, bass swelling it and treble roughening the edges. Leave it off if you are driving a real oscilloscope, because taking the microphone is what collapses the output to mono. Nothing is recorded and nothing leaves your device.

Between the lines the words collapse into a wireframe cube or pyramid and come back out as the next line. The solid is turning in three dimensions, and the beam is drawing every edge of it in order, the same way it draws the letters.
